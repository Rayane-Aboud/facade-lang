FacadeLang

A typed language for façade design. Units, dimensions and kinds of quantity are part of the type, so the slider swap of paper Fig. 5 becomes a compile-time error.

The bug

    

      In paper Fig. 5, a façade panel is built from three unnamed number sliders:
      inset = −1, offset = 0, thickness = 0.9. Connect the inset slider where the
      thickness should go, and Dynamo builds a wrong panel without a word. In FacadeLang
      every input has a name and a type:
    


param inset     : Inset     = -1.0 m
param thickness : Thickness =  0.9 m
let panel = thicken(mid, inset)    -- error[E0102]: expected Thickness, found Inset

    

      Both values are lengths, so a checker with units alone would accept the swap. But an
      inset and a thickness are different kinds of length, just as torque and
      energy are different kinds of N·m. The checker knows this, and rejects the program.
    

  


  

    
The language in one screen


-- domain concepts are declared, not hard-coded (the prelude declares SI and façades)
dimension Length
dimension Area = Length^2
unit m  : Length
unit mm = 0.001 m
kind Thickness : Length                       -- a Length with a specific meaning
extern thicken(Surface, Thickness) -> Solid   -- geometry is opaque: only its type is known

-- a design: named, typed inputs and a dataflow of pure functions
param thickness : Thickness = 0.9 m  where thickness > 0 m
let panel = thicken(mid, thickness)
let vol   = area(mid) * thickness             -- inferred: Length^3


    
    
      
  • Dimensions. +, − and comparisons need equal dimensions (E0101). *, / and ^ combine them, as an abelian group of exponent vectors: L² · L = L³, inferred without annotation.
    • 
      
  • Units are normalised to SI and then erased: 2.5 mm is the number 0.0025 of dimension Length.
    • 
      
  • Kinds form a subtyping hierarchy: Thickness <: Length. A plain 0.9 m receives its kind once, where a name is declared. After that a kind cannot be relabelled, and an Inset never reaches a Thickness parameter (E0102).
    • 
      
  • Guarantee. Well-typed programs are dimensionally homogeneous (Kennedy's dimension types) and kind-safe. The typing rules and proof sketches are in the formal document for Feature 1.
    •
